## Brand & Style
The design system expresses an intimate, romantic, and tactile floral commerce aesthetic. Rooted in artisanal curation, it balances warm organic grace with the polished structure of a high-end luxury atelier. The visual tone is welcoming, lush, and thoughtful, inspiring delight and trust for gift-givers and celebration curators alike.

The aesthetic philosophy draws on contemporary editorial minimalism softened by romantic heritage touches. Clean, creamy canvas tones and airy negative space allow floral photography to take center stage, while subtle accents of deep plum and warm antique gold provide depth and regal elegance. The UI communicates refinement without intimidation—approachable luxury grounded in bespoke floral craftsmanship.

## Colors
The color architecture transitions from radiant botanical freshness to grounded luxury:

- **Primary (`#D84A6A`)**: A refined rose-pink evoking fresh petals and romantic elegance. Used for primary calls-to-action, key badges, and interactive moments.
- **Secondary (`#3E5B42`)**: A timeless, deep forest green referencing natural stems and foliage. Governs secondary interactions, botanical highlights, and trust indicators.
- **Tertiary / Heritage Purple (`#2F1B2E`)**: A deep, imperial plum representing boutique heritage, quiet luxury, and emotional gravitas. Used for primary typography, editorial callouts, and dark footer regions.
- **Neutral (`#FDFBF7`)**: A soft porcelain cream that avoids sterile white, producing an inviting, velvety canvas that complements natural flower photography.
- **Antique Gold (`#C5A059`)**: Reserved for delicate ornamental borders, rating stars, and subtle luxury seals.

## Typography
The typographic system pairs the high-contrast elegance of **Playfair Display** with the clear, humanistic warmth of **Plus Jakarta Sans**.

- **Editorial Headings**: Playfair Display provides poetic literary charm for section headers, collection titles, and romantic quotes.
- **Functional Interface & Body**: Plus Jakarta Sans offers effortless legibility for product descriptions, checkout flows, specifications, and cart details.
- **Eyebrows & Section Markers**: Rendered in uppercase with widened letter-spacing (`0.14em`), anchoring romantic motifs within a disciplined modern architecture.
- **Script Accents**: Where floral script touches are introduced via SVG flourishes or delicate calligraphy accents, they should accompany—not replace—semantic HTML headings.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout relies on a generous 12-column responsive fluid grid anchored by comfortable exterior margins to reinforce a boutique atmosphere.

- **Desktop (>= 1200px)**: 12 columns with `1.5rem` gutters and minimum `3rem` section margins. Maximum content container width caps at `1320px` to maintain optimal viewing proportions for visual arrangements.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px)**: 8 columns with `1.25rem` gutters and `2rem` side padding.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4 columns with `1rem` gutters and `1.25rem` margins. E-commerce cards switch to dual-column grids or horizontal swipe modules to optimize discoverability.
- **Vertical Cadence**: Ample vertical spacing (`space-xl` to `space-xxl`) between sections establishes calm breathing room, preventing the visual clutter typical of discount retail.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is created through subtle layering, parchment-toned overlays, and airy, diffused shadows rather than harsh drop shadows.

- **Surface Layers**: The foundation is a warm cream base (`#FDFBF7`). Raised elements such as product cards and modals sit on pure white (`#FFFFFF`) or tinted champagne (`#F7EFE3`) surfaces.
- **Ambient Diffusion**: Shadows utilize a botanical tint (`rgba(47, 27, 46, 0.05)`) mixed with a soft spread (e.g., `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(47, 27, 46, 0.06)`), mimicking natural afternoon gallery lighting.
- **Fine Borders**: Elevated components and product tiles are framed with hairline borders (`1px solid #EFE8DC`) to preserve shape integrity on lighter displays without adding heavy visual weight.
- **Interactive Hover**: Cards transition on hover with an ultra-smooth Y-axis lift (`-4px`) and a gentle shadow expansion tinted in soft rose-plum.

## Shapes
The shape philosophy favors understated softness (`0.25rem` base, up to `0.5rem` for product tiles and dialogs) with intentional pill elements reserved for active filters and category tags. This keeps the presentation tailored and classic, balancing the organic curves of flora with crisp editorial geometry.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action**: Solid rose (`#D84A6A`) with crisp white typography in `label-lg`, subtle hover darkening (`#C43D5C`), and `rounded-sm` corners.
- **Secondary Action**: Heritage forest green (`#3E5B42`) or outlined in deep plum (`#2F1B2E`) with a 1px border, pairing high editorial contrast with organic stability.
- **Tertiary / Text**: Understated uppercase link styling with an animated hairline underline in antique gold (`#C5A059`).

### Product Cards
- **Architecture**: Enclosed within 1px `#EFE8DC` borders, with an aspect ratio of 4:5 for floral portraiture.
- **Content Hierarchy**: High-resolution image followed by an optional delivery badge (e.g., "Same-Day Delivery"), Playfair Display title, price formatted in `body-md` bold, and a clean "Quick Add" trigger.
- **Hover Behavior**: Subtle zoom (`scale-102`) on the bouquet photograph, revealing a secondary view or quick-view overlay.

### Badges & Chips
- **Aesthetic**: Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`) or soft-rectangle (`rounded-sm`), styled with pale champagne backgrounds (`#F7EFE3`) and plum/forest text.
- **Seasonal / Signature Seals**: Delicate gold border (`1px solid #C5A059`) with uppercase `label-eyebrow` typography.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- **Form Controls**: Crisp 1px border in `#EFE8DC`, transitioning to forest green (`#3E5B42`) or rose on active focus, set against pure white backgrounds.
- **Date & Delivery Pickers**: Calendar cards featuring rounded date pills, clear slot statuses, and delivery notes styled as reassuring micro-copy.

### Notification Banners
- **Top Announcement Bar**: Subtle warm gold-rose gradient or solid heritage plum with centered `body-sm` text informing customers of shipping areas, delivery deadlines, and bespoke concierge inquiries.
